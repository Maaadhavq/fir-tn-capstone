## Questions the panel is likely to ask — and the one-line answers

- *Why does the classifier find nothing on your own theft sample?* Domain shift (finding 5): it is
  trained on ILSI court fact-statements; its top-5 for that complaint does not contain theft at
  all, so a lower threshold would apply wrong sections. In-domain synthetic data is the fix; the
  cue scan carries short inputs meanwhile — and it is shown as a question, never applied.
- *Why TF-IDF and not the legal BERT you proposed?* Measured on the full corpus: 0.700 vs 0.526
  micro-F1 with every lever applied; ensemble +0.0006. The encoder's job is element verification.
- *Is 51% WER usable?* Not as a transcript to file — as decision support with provenance, with
  CER 15% (Tamil word boundaries inflate WER), with fabrication guards, and with IndicWhisper /
  a code-switch fine-tune as the planned next steps. The number is honest and zero-shot.
- *Who signs off the legal tables?* Nobody yet: parsed from the gazette with page citations,
  spot-checked by 25 tests, awaiting a legal read-through — the form says so.
- *What is "conditional"?* The Schedule's own wording: abetment follows the offence, s.85 depends on
  who reports, theft splits at ₹5,000. It routes to the officer and quotes the condition.
- *Can the system file an FIR?* No path exists: no endpoint, CLI or node sets `verified`; tests
  fail if one appears.
